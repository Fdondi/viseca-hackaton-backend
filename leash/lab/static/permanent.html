a customer's standing rules, read from their profile: they apply to every mandate
Input · the profile (customers.csv), editable
Edit any field: the rules update as you type. Try “avoids gift vouchers and energy drinks”, “no subscriptions”, or “Regular trips to Spain”.
How it's read

Fixed patterns only, no model: a rule that applies to every purchase must trace to exact words. Only three kinds of statement become rules: prohibitions (“avoids…”, “no…”) → hard rules; where you shop and travel → a shop elsewhere asks you, never declines; a careful budget style → ask when unsure. Everything else is noted, not enforced, with the reason.
